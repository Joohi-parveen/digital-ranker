What AI Search Optimization Services Do We Offer?

Technical AI SEO

What is GEO?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) concentrates on the optimization of your brand, content, and data in the AI-driven answers. It allows the AI platforms to better understand your business and increases the likelihood of your content getting referenced or recommended by AI algorithms.

What is AEO?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) focuses on optimising content so search engines and AI-powered platforms can easily understand, extract, and present it as a direct answer to users’ questions.
